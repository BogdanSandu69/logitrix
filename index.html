How to Play

  1. Choose a difficulty and press PLAY
  2. Select a letter from the bottom panel (or press 1–6)
  3. Click grid cells to place letters
  4. Click again with the same letter to clear the cell
  5. Satisfy all rules to win — use 💡 Hint if stuck
Press 1–4 to pick difficulty  ·  Enter to start
